System Types
Eyebrow: System types
Title: On-grid, off-grid or hybrid?
Description: Choose a system to see how energy flows from your panels to your property, the grid and batteries.
System	Headline	Description	Suited For
On-Grid	Solar with the grid	Panels power your property during the day through an inverter. The grid supplies power when solar is not enough, and net metering records the energy exchanged with it.	Properties with a dependable grid connection.
Off-Grid	Solar with storage, independent of the grid	Panels charge batteries through the inverter, and the stored energy powers your property when the sun is down. There is no grid connection.	Locations without a dependable grid supply.
Hybrid	Grid-connected, with battery backup	Solar powers your property and charges batteries. The grid stays connected, and stored energy takes over during outages.	Properties that want solar plus backup during power cuts.
